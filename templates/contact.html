Choose the right path

  • Message issue: use the Report message control beside the public message when possible.
  • Payment or refund: contact support before opening a payment dispute when practical so we can investigate the transaction.
  • Privacy request: identify the request as a privacy matter and use the email associated with your account.
  • Urgent safety concern: Take the Board is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services for an immediate threat.
