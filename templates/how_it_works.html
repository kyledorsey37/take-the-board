Fair play, built in

The board moves when the payment settles.

We check the board again when a move is ready to settle. If another fan got there first, your move does not take the board—and you are not charged for a takeover you did not win.

When someone challenges you

The rivalry keeps going.

Their message replaces yours, your move stays in the takeover history, and you can always come back to make another move.

Keep it fierce. Keep it in bounds.

Rivalry is the point. Crossing the line is not.

Trash talk and school pride belong here. Threats, slurs, personal information, targeted harassment, and links do not. Messages are checked before they go live.

What control means

A spot on the board—not a stake in a school.

Take the Board control is a status inside the game. It does not mean you own a university, represent a team, or are affiliated with any school.

Straight answers

The fine print, without the fog.

How long does my message stay up?

Every successful takeover is guaranteed to stay on the board for at least 30 seconds. After that, it remains live until another successful bid takes the board, the message is removed for breaking the guidelines, or the current game week ends.

What am I paying for?

You are paying for temporary control of a public message in an independent fan rivalry game, including a guaranteed 30-second display window—not school ownership, investment, sponsorship, or a fixed amount of time beyond that window.

What happens at the end of the week?

The boards reset for a fresh round of rivalry. Your takeover history remains part of the game’s record, and every fan gets a new chance to start the next fight.

Is Take the Board affiliated with a school?

No. Take the Board is independent fan entertainment and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any university, athletic department, conference, or the NCAA.
